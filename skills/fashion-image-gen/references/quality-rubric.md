# Fashion Quality Rubric

Check every generated board before returning it.

## Product Fidelity

- silhouette remains close to the reference item
- seams, closures, and construction feel plausible
- visible design details are not invented away

## Material Realism

- fabric drape looks believable
- texture and sheen match the implied material
- prints or knit structures are not corrupted

## Marketing Usefulness

- the product is easy to see
- the framing matches the requested mode
- the image feels usable for campaign, buyer review, or catalog draft

## Human Realism

- pose is natural
- hands and limbs are intact
- the garment sits on the body in a believable way

## Reject Conditions

Regenerate if any of these appear:

- broken anatomy
- melted garment edges
- unreadable logo or label artifacts
- scene overwhelms the product
- output mode mismatch
