# Fashion Workflow Modes

## `model-styling`

Use when the user wants the product shown on a model in a polished marketing visual.

Defaults:

- framing: `three-quarter`
- background: `soft-neutral-studio`
- tone: `commercial`
- count: `4`

Best for:

- designers needing quick on-model concepts
- buyers needing stronger visual sell-in than raw product photos

## `campaign`

Use when the user wants aspirational, styled, ad-like imagery.

Defaults:

- framing: `full-body`
- background: `luxury-interior`
- tone: `editorial`
- count: `4`

Best for:

- launch concepts
- lookbooks
- mood-forward brand storytelling

## `product-detail`

Use when the user wants a cleaner commercial image that keeps focus on the product.

Defaults:

- framing: `detail-close-up`
- background: `white-studio`
- tone: `commercial`
- count: `4`

Best for:

- ecommerce
- catalog drafts
- buyer reviews

## `variant-preview`

Use when the user wants controlled exploration of alternate colors, materials, or light styling changes.

Defaults:

- framing: `three-quarter`
- background: `soft-neutral-studio`
- tone: `balanced`
- count: `4`

Best for:

- design iteration
- buyer discussion
- merchandising reviews
