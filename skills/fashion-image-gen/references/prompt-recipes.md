# Fashion Prompt Recipes

## Prompt Structure

Assemble prompts in this order:

1. workflow goal
2. product identity
3. image composition
4. style direction
5. realism and preservation constraints

Keep the product identity and preservation instructions explicit.

## Core Constraints

Include these in most prompts:

- preserve the garment's core silhouette and recognizable construction
- keep fabric behavior believable
- avoid distorted logos, labels, hardware, and prints
- keep the product visible and marketable
- maintain natural pose, hands, and garment interaction

## `model-styling` Recipe

Structure:

- polished fashion marketing image
- model wearing the referenced product
- chosen framing and background
- chosen preset and tone
- strong garment-preservation constraints

## `campaign` Recipe

Structure:

- aspirational campaign image featuring the referenced product
- stronger scene and mood language
- keep product identifiable even in editorial styling

## `product-detail` Recipe

Structure:

- clean, retail-ready image centered on the referenced product
- minimal distractions
- lighting that flatters fabric and construction details

## `variant-preview` Recipe

Structure:

- preserve shape and construction
- change only approved attributes such as color, finish, or styling level
- keep scene and framing controlled
